From project scope to finishing details

The process starts with a conversation about your goals and the parts of the home involved. From there, the scope can be clarified, key decisions identified, and the sequence of work discussed. For a kitchen or bath, that may mean defining which features are being remodeled. For a deck, roof, or new construction project, the work will have a different scope and planning needs.

A few terms you may hear:
- Scope: The agreed description of the work included in the project.
- Estimate: A projected cost based on the information available about the work.
- Change order: A documented change to the agreed scope, which may affect cost or timing.

Keep a written record of selections and requested changes, and ask how each one affects the plan. The general contractor remodeling near me you choose should be able to discuss the project in terms you understand.
